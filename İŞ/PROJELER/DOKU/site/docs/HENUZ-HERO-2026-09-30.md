## Tam duvar istemi

Use case: product-mockup. Create one original museum-calibre abstract wall composition in a real-scale architectural interior, for DOKU website hero. NEW INVENTION ONLY, no references, no imitation of artists, no known artwork or style signature. Working title Henüz, no words in the image. Artistic intent: an enigmatic arrangement that feels like a forgotten sign, viewers search for a meaning but there is no assigned meaning, no actual readable symbol, no clear face or representational subject. It must have commanding, unsettlingly balanced artistic presence, precise composition and visual silence rather than generic decorative blobs. Invent a continuous planar tessellation covering ONE entire 4.8 m wide by 2.8 m tall wall, floor to ceiling and side to side, made from about 20 LARGE bespoke CNC-cut MDF pieces. Entire front surface of ALL pieces is on exactly ONE plane and identical thickness 8mm, NOT a relief sculpture: no bent panels, folds, bulges, overlap, stacking, inset levels or varied depth. Flat broad faces with softly radiused wrapped edges. Every shape and every colour tone is its own physically separate upholstered piece, separated from neighbours by consistent narrow 3mm dark open joints. The background is also separate large flat ivory upholstered panels, not plaster. Architecture of the composition: an off-axis upright almost-gate occupies the centre-right, its top breaks before closing; the left half is a dense tall tobacco and oxblood counterform with a broad ivory inward bite; a midnight indigo blunt wedge interrupts the lower middle, an isolated pale clay oblong balances the right. Thick blunt interrupted paths almost connect across the wall, inviting the eye to invent relationships. Make all these as interlocking tessellated areas, NOT shapes laid on top of a background. Asymmetric rhythm of large concave and convex cuts, off-axis straight cuts, quiet irrational junctions. No circles, concentric arches, waves, ribbons, flowers, faces, figures, lettering or fashionable organic-blob mosaic. Restrained palette: warm bone ivory majority, deep oxblood, tobacco brown, midnight indigo, pale clay; each panel strictly ONE solid colour, no gradient, no painted shading, no printed art. Actual very fine matte leather and high-quality woven fabric over thin MDF, faint real material grain visible at close viewing, no seams or stitching, glue marks, wrinkles, swelling, puffy cushion appearance, plastic or gloss. All panel widths substantial and easy to wrap, smallest piece at least 150mm across; no hairline motifs, acute tips or fragile slivers. Only real ambient lighting may create very subtle light variation, no exaggerated shadow relief. Photograph straight-on, landscape 3:2, showing whole wall at correct scale, luxury but restrained real living room: a low neutral sofa on far left occupying less than one quarter of wall width, dark low table and quiet pale floor. Furniture subordinate, main mysterious composition entirely visible in central 78% width and upper 75% height so a wide hero crop works. Soft diffuse side daylight, accurate architectural perspective, realistic material, crisp refined editorial photography. One scene only. No collage, inset, text, logo, watermark or people. Prioritize artistically arresting unfamiliar composition AND faithful flat coplanar manufacturable construction.
