## Üretim istemi

Use case: product-mockup. Create ONE original high-end interior concept image for DOKU, a bespoke sculptural wall-design brand. Asset: wide landscape website hero, 3:2 image, no typography. Design title for our internal use only: Eşik (Threshold), DO NOT render words. A restrained contemporary living room, photographed straight-on from a generous distance with natural architectural proportions. The subject is an exceptionally beautiful original large sculptural wall composition, occupying the upper middle 65% of the frame on a pale warm grey plaster wall. Invent a sophisticated continuous rhythm of six large interlocking, smoothly folded ribbon-like relief panels: one long pale ivory arc gently crosses a deep desaturated petrol-green vertical fold, soft chalk and olive-grey planes turn outwards at differing shallow depths; a fine dark green shadow seam weaves between planes. The silhouette is asymmetric and elegant, like a horizon opening through folds, with a deliberately airy centre and precise softly rounded edges. This is tactile dimensional relief, not printed wallpaper, not a painting, not loose rounded blobs, not circles or a flower. Palette: predominately warm ivory/chalk, desaturated deep petrol green and restrained olive-grey, only one small warm clay plane. The invented wall design must feel distinctive, resolved, visually balanced, and memorable. There is subtle microtexture on all sculptural surfaces, refined narrow seams, convincing occlusion and long soft side-lit shadows, no shiny gold. The complete design is visible with margins at both sides and above. Room: a low quiet light stone-coloured sofa in the lower foreground, a minimal dark smoked-wood coffee table, one delicate branch at far left; few accessories, no clutter. Room furnishing should support the wall, not steal focus. Soft late-afternoon daylight entering from the left, beautifully controlled contrast, crisp highly realistic interior editorial photography, premium calming atmosphere. Composition must work in both a 1.8:1 desktop crop and a 1.35:1 mobile crop: keep the entire important relief composition in the central 78% of image width, wall art entirely in upper 75% of image height; no important objects at edges. No people, no text, no labels, no logo, no watermark, no website UI, no references to existing designers or copied branded products. Produce a polished final asset, not a sketch or moodboard.
